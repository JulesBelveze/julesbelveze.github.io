## Your instruction-tuned model passes the vibe check. But does it actually work?

Standard benchmarks won't cut it when evaluating instruction-following capabilities. This article dives into specialized metrics like instruction relevance scoring, multi-turn coherence, and robustness testing that actually measure what matters.

Plus: learn how to update only 0.1% of your model's parameters using techniques like LoRA and adapters without sacrificing performance, and discover continual learning strategies that prevent your model from forgetting everything when you add new capabilities.

If you need to evaluate production agents rigorously or train efficiently at scale, these techniques are non-negotiable.
